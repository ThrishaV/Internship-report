# Cyber Crime Police Station Internship

## About the Internship

This repository contains a short overview of my internship at **Cyber Crime Police Station, Ernakulam Rural, Aluva, Kerala**, from **1 June 2026 to 15 June 2026**.

As a B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering (Cybersecurity) student at Muthoot Institute of Technology and Science, this internship gave me an opportunity to learn how cybercrime investigations are carried out in real life.

I got to understand different types of cybercrimes and how digital evidence, forensic tools, and investigation techniques are used to solve cases.

## What I Learned

During the internship, I got exposure to the following areas:

### 1. Cybercrime Investigation
- Learned how different cybercrime complaints are handled.
- Understood the basic steps involved in investigating online fraud and cyber harassment.
- Observed how digital evidence helps in investigations.

### 2. Telecom Analysis
- Learned about CDR and IPDR analysis.
- Understood IMEI, IMSI, Cell ID, and tower details.
- Learned how telecom information can help trace suspicious activities.

### 3. OSINT (Open Source Intelligence)
- Explored tools like WhatsMyName, Have I Been Pwned, C-Trace, and Khoj.
- Learned how publicly available information can be used during investigations.
- Understood digital footprints and online identity tracking.

### 4. Digital Forensics
- Learned how digital evidence is collected and preserved.
- Got exposure to Cell Grabber and Tableau TD2.
- Understood forensic imaging, hashing, and chain of custody.

### 5. Legal Aspects
- Learned about the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023.
- Understood the importance of maintaining the integrity of electronic evidence.
- Learned about Sections 61–63 related to electronic records.

### 6. Digital Awareness
- Attended an awareness session on digital well-being and mobile phone usage.
- Learned about responsible internet usage and online safety.

## Tools I Explored


 Cell Grabber : Mobile data extraction 
 Tableau TD2 : Forensic imaging 
 WHOIS : Finding domain registration details 
 WhatsMyName : Searching for online usernames 
 Have I Been Pwned : Checking email data breach exposure 
 C-Trace and Khoj : Exploring information for investigations 
 Hashing : Checking whether digital evidence has been changed 

## Types of Cases I Learned About

I got to learn about different real-world cybercrime cases, such as:

- Online financial fraud
- Social media impersonation
- Cyberstalking and online harassment
- Bank account misuse
- WhatsApp job scams
- Forex investment fraud
- Fake Facebook profiles

These cases helped me understand how the concepts I learn in cybersecurity are used in actual investigations.

## My Experience

This internship was a valuable learning experience for me. It helped me connect what I study in college with how cybersecurity works in the real world.

I gained a better understanding of digital forensics, OSINT, telecom analysis, and cybercrime investigation. It also gave me an opportunity to explore tools and techniques that I had mostly learned about in theory.

I am grateful to the officers and staff at Cyber Crime Police Station, Aluva, for their guidance and for giving me this opportunity to learn.
